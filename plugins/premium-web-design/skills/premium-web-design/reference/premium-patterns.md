# Premium App Design Patterns

Five patterns that elevate UI from generic to premium.

---

## Pattern #1: Interactions & Animations

Add animation to page changes (sliding transitions rather than instant fades). For key features, design multi-part animations (button rotating into checkmark, background expanding). These micro-interactions make the app feel polished and satisfying.

**Why:** Animations signal thoughtful craft, not template generation. They provide feedback and create emotional engagement.

---

## Pattern #2: Custom Illustrations

Replace generic "No data found" text with custom illustrations or animated mascots. Turn frustration moments (empty screens) into moments of delight. Use a base illustration and feed it into image generation AI for infinite variations (mascot sleeping, searching, drinking coffee).

**Why:** Empty states, error messages, and loading screens are where generic apps show their seams.

---

## Pattern #3: Haptic & Sensory Feedback

**Mobile:** Apply subtle haptic vibrations to interactions. Vary intensity by action importance (light for common taps, heavier for critical actions).

**Web:** Translate to immediate visual feedback (button states, hover effects) and subtle auditory feedback where appropriate.

**Why:** Multi-sensory experience makes interactions feel "real" and distinguishes from flat, unresponsive interfaces.

---

## Pattern #4: Consistent & Thoughtful Icons

- Avoid default icon packs — use quality ones (Hero Icons, Lucide, Phosphor, Radix)
- Once a style is chosen (thin lines vs filled), stick to it rigorously
- Use style variations to indicate state (outline = inactive, filled = active)

**Why:** Inconsistent iconography is one of the fastest signals of a rushed, template-based design.

**Recommended packs:**
- Hero Icons (heroicons.com) — Clean, modern, MIT
- Lucide (lucide.dev) — Fork of Feather with more variety
- Phosphor (phosphoricons.com) — Six weights, highly customizable
- Radix Icons (radix-ui.com) — Simple, matches Radix UI

---

Don't just consume — analyze why certain designs work.
