# Landing Structure — Conversion Page Anatomy

Section architecture for high-converting marketing pages. Use with `--create --landing` or whenever the deliverable is a landing page, product page, or campaign page. This reference covers *structure and psychology*; for the copy itself, apply the a copywriting skill skill (AIDA, StoryBrand, PAS); for scroll effects, see [overdrive narrative structure](overdrive.md).

---

## The Scroll Journey

Each section answers ONE psychological question the visitor is silently asking, in the order they ask it. Skipping a question (or answering out of order) is where pages leak conversions.

| # | Section | Visitor's silent question | Job | Animation role (if any) |
|---|---------|--------------------------|-----|------------------------|
| 1 | **Hero** | "Am I in the right place?" | What/who/why in 3 seconds + one primary CTA | The showpiece — establish tone, stop the scroll |
| 2 | **Problem** | "Do they understand my situation?" | Name the pain specifically enough that the visitor feels seen | Subtle reveal that gives the pain weight |
| 3 | **Solution** | "How does this fix it?" | The mechanism, framed before/after | Scroll-linked demo or transition showing the change |
| 4 | **Features / How it works** | "What exactly do I get?" | 3-5 capabilities tied to outcomes (not a spec dump) | Staggered entrances aid scanning; nothing more |
| 5 | **Social proof** | "Who else trusts this?" | Logos, numbers, recognizable names | None or minimal — trust content must be static and effortless to read |
| 6 | **Testimonials** | "People like me?" | 1-3 specific stories with names, faces, and a concrete result | None (no auto-carousels — they kill readability) |
| 7 | **Pricing** | "Can I afford it? Which option?" | Clear tiers with ONE emphasized recommendation | Micro-interaction on tier selection only |
| 8 | **FAQ** | "What's the catch?" | Preempt the 4-6 real objections (not filler questions) | Smooth accordion expand, nothing else |
| 9 | **Final CTA** | "OK — what do I do now?" | One action, restated value, zero new information | One focused moment on the CTA itself |

**Not every page needs all 9.** Cut sections when the question is already answered (a free tool may not need pricing or FAQ); never reorder past proof → ask.

## Rules

### CTA discipline
- ONE primary CTA per section, repeated consistently (same verb, same destination) at hero, after proof, and at the close
- Secondary actions are ghost/text links, never competing buttons
- Above the fold: low-friction ask (see demo, try free) — never a paywall or long form before value is shown

### Psychology sequencing
- **Problem before solution** — a solution presented before the pain is established reads as a feature list
- **Proof before pricing** — the visitor must believe it works before being asked to pay
- **Specificity converts**: "Send 10,000 personalized emails in minutes" beats "Transform your workflow" in every section, not just the hero
- Address the visitor's stage: cold traffic needs sections 1-6 fully developed; warm/retargeted traffic can compress 2-4

### Where animation helps vs hurts
- **Helps**: hero tone-setting, demonstrating the product transformation (section 3), guiding the eye through step sequences
- **Hurts**: anything moving inside proof/testimonials/pricing, auto-advancing carousels, scroll-hijacking near the CTA
- Apply the descending-intensity rule from [overdrive](overdrive.md): spectacle at the top, clarity at the bottom

### Layout anti-slop (per main SKILL.md)
- No 3-column equal card grids for features — use 2-column zig-zag, bento, or asymmetric layouts
- No centered-everything — asymmetry reads as designed
- Pricing: emphasize ONE tier clearly (size, border, position), not three equal towers

## Pre-Ship Checks

- [ ] 3-second test passes on the hero (what / who / why)
- [ ] Each section answers its question from the table — read the page as the skeptical visitor
- [ ] One primary CTA path, consistent verb, no competing buttons
- [ ] Proof is specific (names, numbers, logos) — no fake round numbers
- [ ] FAQ answers real objections (price, effort, risk, alternatives)
- [ ] Copy reviewed against a copywriting skill frameworks
- [ ] Run the [pre-launch performance gate](audit.md) before publishing
